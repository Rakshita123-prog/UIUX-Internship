# UI/UX Design Projects — GlowLogics Internship

## About

This repository contains the UI/UX design projects I am working on as part of my online internship at **GlowLogics**. Through these projects, I am gaining hands-on experience in designing user-friendly interfaces, organizing layouts, and improving the overall user experience.

## Projects

### 1. Food Ordering App

**Description:**

The Food Ordering App is a UI/UX design project focused on making it easy for users to explore food items, browse menus, and place orders through a simple and user-friendly interface.

**Key Features:**

* User-friendly food browsing experience
* Food item and menu displays
* Clean and organized interface
* Easy navigation between screens
* Focus on a smooth ordering experience

**Tools Used:** Figma

### 2. Online Shopping Mart Website

**Status:** In Progress

**Description:**

The Online Shopping Mart Website is an ongoing UI/UX design project focused on creating a convenient online shopping experience. The goal is to design an organized and visually appealing website where users can explore products and navigate through the shopping process easily.

**Planned Features:**

* Product listing and browsing
* Product categories
* Product details
* Shopping cart interface
* Simple and intuitive navigation
* Responsive website layout

**Tools Used:** Figma

*Note: Features may be updated as the project progresses.*
